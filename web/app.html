EV calculator

Price an entry from sportsbook odds, see its expected value, and get a stake that fits your limits.

For each leg, enter the sharp book's over and under odds at the PrizePicks line, or type a fair % directly. The example values are placeholders; replace them with your own.

Fair win % is the sharp book's price with the vig removed. If you type a fair %, it is used instead of the sharp odds.

Bankroll & settings

Your limits drive every suggested stake and the warnings on the dashboard. Changes save as you go.

Bankroll and staking

Bankroll here is your starting amount. The app adds your settled profit and loss to it. If you deposit or withdraw, change the starting amount by the same figure.

PrizePicks payouts

Multipliers by number of hits. Check these against the PrizePicks app; they change from time to time and can differ by state. Format: hits:multiplier, separated by commas.

Other apps and custom entries

Add Underdog, Sleeper, or a PrizePicks entry with goblins or demons. Copy the payouts exactly as the app shows them before you submit. Entries in the same group step down to a smaller entry when a leg is voided; otherwise a void returns your stake.

Backup

Your bets are saved on this phone or computer only. Download a backup now and then; to move your bets to another device, or bring them back after clearing your browser, load the backup under Today's picks → Load a file or restore a backup.

People

Everyone who has opened Edge Finder. Each person gets their own private bankroll and bet log; nobody, you included, can see anyone else's bets. Only you can load new picks.

Add someone

  1. Open this page's Share menu.
  2. Invite them by email and set them to Editor. People outside your Claude organization can only save their own bets at Editor, and only while the page isn't shared by a public link, so leave link sharing off.
  3. They sign in with their own Claude account and show up here the first time they open the app.

To remove someone, take them off in the Share menu. Editors can also change the page itself, so only invite people you trust.
